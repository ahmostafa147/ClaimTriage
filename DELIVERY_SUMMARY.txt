================================================================================
WHY LANDINGAI AND PATHWAY
================================================================================

LandingAI ADE:
- Handles noisy scanned documents
- Returns bounding boxes for evidence chain
- Field-level confidence scores
- Critical for audit trail

Pathway:
- Real-time streaming for live processing
- Sub-second routing updates
- Scales to 1000s of documents/hour
- Live index with low latency

Without these: 30-40% field miss rate, no bboxes, batch delays
